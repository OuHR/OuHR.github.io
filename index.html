Haoran Ou

PhD Student, Nanyang Technological University

I am a PhD student in Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Tianwei Zhang. My research interests are in AI safety and security, especially LLM and agent security, web-augmented models, multimodal fact-checking, and trustworthy AI systems.

Before starting my PhD, I worked on web security and privacy, including GDPR compliance analysis, fraud website detection, and practical security measurement.
